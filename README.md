Sustain Energy – Graded Unit 2 Web Application

Project Overview
Sustain Energy is a sustainability‑focused web application developed for the HN Software Development Graded Unit 2 assessment at Edinburgh College. The system enables organisations to register, authenticate, record sustainability activities, calculate environmental performance scores, and access information relating to sustainable practices. The project aligns with recognised sustainability frameworks and promotes responsible corporate behaviour through accurate reporting and transparent data handling.

The application has been designed with an emphasis on usability, accessibility, and consistent interface design across all pages.

---

Mission Statement
The purpose of Sustain Energy is to support companies in adopting sustainable practices and improving their environmental performance. The system encourages accurate reporting, responsible decision‑making, and continuous improvement by providing tools that measure sustainability activities and highlight areas for development. The platform promotes the principles of good corporate citizenship and environmental accountability.

---

Functional Requirements

1. Registration and Authentication
- New company registration using an email address and nominated password.
- Secure login functionality for returning users.
- Password recovery mechanism for users who have forgotten their credentials.

2. User Information Management
- A dedicated page displaying company details, including:
  - Company name  
  - Contact person  
  - Telephone number  
  - Join date  
  - Account status (active, inactive, deactivated)
- CRUD functionality for updating or managing user information.

3. Informational and Mission Pages
- A mission statement outlining the purpose of the website.
- A sustainability information section referencing UNESCO guidance.
- Promotional content encouraging sustainable choices and responsible behaviour.

4. Green Calculator (Sustainability Rubric)
- A rubric‑based scoring system that calculates sustainability points based on company activities.
- Annual activity selection and scoring.
- Dashboard updates showing total accrued points.
- Support for purchasing additional points when required.

5. Shopping Trolley System
- A purchasing system allowing companies to buy green vouchers.
- Designed to cover shortfalls between required sustainability targets and achieved points.

6. Feedback and Communication
- A feedback page enabling users to contact the site administrator.
- Includes fields for name, email, contact number, and comments.

7. Visual and Design Standards
- Consistent screen design across all pages.
- Accessibility features including adjustable font size and responsive layout.
- Sustainability‑themed images and custom approval logos.

8. Legal and Regulatory Content
- Privacy and copyright information.
- Terms and conditions outlining accuracy requirements for company reporting.
- Emphasis on regulatory compliance and responsible data submission.
