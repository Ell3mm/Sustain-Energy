Regulatory Conditions

  • Companies must provide accurate, verifiable information about sustainability activities.
  • Any audit or review requests must be supported with evidence (e.g., invoices, reports, logs).
  • False or misleading claims may lead to account deactivation or loss of points.

Good Corporate Citizenship

We encourage honest reporting because it strengthens trust, protects brand reputation, and shows leadership in responsible business practice.

Organisations that maintain accurate records may receive recognition and improved public standing.
