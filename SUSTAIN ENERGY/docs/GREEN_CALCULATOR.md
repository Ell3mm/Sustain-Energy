# Green Calculator Rubric (Starter Version)

This rubric is a placeholder and can be adjusted to match the official
"Sustainability Rubric INFORMATION - Graded Unit 2" document.

## How points are awarded
Each category is scored at one of four levels:
- Not started (0 points)
- Basic steps (5 points)
- Solid progress (10 points)
- Leading practice (15 points)

## Categories
- Energy efficiency
- Renewable energy use
- Waste and recycling
- Water conservation
- Sustainable transport
- Responsible procurement
- Staff engagement
- Community impact

## Output
The total points are displayed on the Green Calculator page and saved to the Dashboard.
Purchased points from the Green Shop are added to the total balance.
