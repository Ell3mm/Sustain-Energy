Sustainability Description

Last updated: 01/05/2026

This promotional section highlights the parts of sustainability that the platform supports. It aligns with broad UNESCO-aligned themes like climate action, responsible consumption, and community wellbeing, while remaining focused on practical actions for businesses.

Energy and Emissions

Reduce energy use, move toward renewables, and improve efficiency year on year.

Waste and Resources

Prioritise reuse, recycling, and responsible procurement across departments.

Water Stewardship

Protect water resources through measurement, conservation, and leak control.

People and Community

Support staff engagement, wellbeing, and positive local impact.
